    On the name

    A field is where fireflies come out at night, and where naturalists go to watch them. In algebra it is a set of numbers closed under addition and multiplication, in which every number except zero can be divided by: the rationals, the reals, the complex numbers. In physics it is a quantity spread through space. And in this first issue, every firefly answers to the mean field of all the others.
